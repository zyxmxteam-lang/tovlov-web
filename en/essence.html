Frequently asked questions

Before you begin.

Do I need to apply before paying?

Every membership starts with a private application and an initial screening. That’s how we protect the quality of the community and make sure the level you choose is right for where you are.

Can I switch levels later?

Yes. All levels share the same method; you can move up whenever you’re ready, guided by your team.

How confidential is it?

Completely. There are no public profiles. Only your matchmaking team knows your information, and it’s never shared without your explicit permission.

Do you guarantee I’ll find a partner?

No honest service can guarantee love. What we do guarantee is a serious, human and personalized process that raises the quality of every connection.
