What is matchmaking? And why it’s making a comeback.

For centuries, someone you trusted introduced two people worth introducing. Apps replaced them with an algorithm. Today, thousands of people tired of swiping are returning to what always worked: a human filter.

Your grandmother probably didn’t meet your grandfather by chance. Someone introduced them: an aunt, a friend, the neighbor who “knew a very decent young man.” Add method and professionalism, and that’s matchmaking.

What matchmaking is, in one sentence

Matchmaking is a personalized service for finding a partner in which a professional, the matchmaker, gets to know you in depth, understands what you’re looking for and why, and only introduces you to people who went through the same screening and are compatible with you in values, lifestyle and relationship vision.

It’s not a random dating agency or an app with coaching. It’s a process where human judgment replaces the algorithm.

An age-old tradition with a new face

Introducing people with intention is one of the oldest social practices there is. Almost every culture has had its matchmaker: in many Mexican families it was an aunt or a godmother; in Jewish tradition, the shadchan is still alive today. What changed isn’t the idea, it’s the method: modern professional matchmaking combines in-depth interviews, relationship psychology and complete confidentiality.

Why it’s coming back right now

Because apps solved the problem of access, but not the problem of connection. The data is clear:

39%of heterosexual couples in the US met online (2017)
78%of app users have felt burned out
40%say what wears them out most is not making a real connection

Having thousands of profiles available doesn’t guarantee finding someone to build a life with. If anything, more options tend to bring more indecision. Matchmaking flips the equation: fewer people, better chosen.

How it works, step by step

Every service has its own method. Here’s TovLov’s:

  1. You apply. A short, private application that we review by hand. We don’t accept everyone: we look for a real intention to build a relationship.
  2. We get to know you. A 1:1 interview with your matchmaker about your story, your context, your values and what you’re no longer willing to repeat.
  3. We define your relationship profile. Together we understand your patterns and define your connection criteria: what’s non-negotiable and what isn’t.
  4. We select. Your matchmaker filters with judgment, not algorithms, among people who also went through the process.
  5. We introduce you. Every person arrives with context and purpose. Fewer dates, better dates.
  6. We support you. Before, during and after every encounter, fine-tuning the direction with each experience.

Matchmaking vs. dating apps

Who is matchmaking for?

  • People who know what they want and have no time to waste on directionless dates.
  • Professionals, entrepreneurs and people with demanding schedules.
  • People who value privacy and don’t want a public profile.
  • People coming out of a long relationship who want to choose again with clarity.
  • People who are tired of apps and want a human, serious process.

No honest service can guarantee love. Be wary of anyone who does. What a good process does guarantee is seriousness, human judgment, confidentiality and a reason behind every introduction. The rest, the chemistry, the decision, the “yes,” is still yours. As it should be.

Frequently asked questions

What does a matchmaker do?

They interview you in depth, understand your story, your values and what you’re looking for, define your connection criteria with you, use human judgment to select people who passed the same screening, introduce them to you with context and support you before, during and after every encounter.

How is matchmaking different from a dating app?

It comes down to who decides. On an app, an algorithm shows you profiles based on photos and proximity; in matchmaking, a human team gets to know you in an interview and only introduces you to people aligned with you. And there are no public profiles.
