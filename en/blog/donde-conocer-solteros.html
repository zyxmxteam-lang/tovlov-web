Where to meet singles who actually want something real.

There are almost 30 million singles in Mexico. The challenge isn’t that they exist, it’s finding the ones who want the same thing you do. Here’s our honest guide to every option.

By TovLov TeamOCT 16, 20268 min readDating

The essentials in 30 seconds
  • Mexico has around 29.6 million single people aged 15 and over, and Mexico City has the highest share in the country.
  • Traditional routes (school, work, friends) lose steam after 30.
  • The best strategy combines 2 or 3 channels that filter for intention, instead of relying on a single app.
  • Matchmaking is the most efficient option for people who want a serious relationship and are short on time.

“I just don’t meet anyone new anymore.” It’s the line we hear most from people between 30 and 50. Your friends are all coupled up, you don’t want to mix work and dating, and the apps have worn you out. So where are the singles who actually want something serious?

First, some context: they exist (and there are lots of them)

According to INEGI, 29.6% of people aged 15 and over are single, and another 15.4% are separated, divorced or widowed. Between 30 and 59, nearly 3 in 10 people don’t live with a partner. Mexico City, Jalisco and Querétaro have some of the highest shares of singles in the country. For the full numbers, read how many singles there are in Mexico.

The problem isn’t supply. It’s that not every route filters for intention.

The 6 routes, honestly assessed

1. Friends and family: the classic that runs dry

For decades it was the main way to meet a partner. Its strength is trust: someone who knows you acts as a filter. Its limit is that, over the years, your network starts repeating itself. Tip: say it out loud. Many people don’t introduce you to anyone simply because they don’t know you’re looking.

2. Dating apps: volume without a filter

They’re the most widely used route (in the US, 39% of heterosexual couples met online), but also the most draining: 78% of users report burnout. They work best with clear rules. Read our guide to dating app burnout.

3. Hobbies, sports and classes

Running, climbing, cooking, dancing or taking a class puts you in front of people with shared interests, in a relaxed setting. The catch: you don’t know who’s single or who wants something serious.

4. Events and trips for singles

This is where the game changes: everyone there is single and knows why they came. The key is choosing curated experiences, with a like-minded guest profile and a format that makes real conversation easy, not parties where nobody talks. TovLov events and TovLov trips are designed for exactly that.

5. Private communities

A club with an entry screening gives you something no app can: continuity. You see the same people several times, trust builds and chemistry shows up without pressure. That’s the model behind TovLov Club.

6. Professional matchmaking

It’s the most efficient option for people who know what they want. A human team interviews you, understands your values, lifestyle and relationship vision, and only introduces you to people who passed the same screening. Fewer dates, better dates. Explore the Essence, Alignment and Signature memberships.

The strategy we recommend

  1. Combine channels: one for volume (friends or hobbies), one in person with a filter (events or a club) and, if you want something serious soon, matchmaking.
  2. Prepare before you go out: get clear on what you want and what you’ll never accept again. Our courses are a great first step.
  3. Measure by quality, not quantity: one good date a month beats ten out of obligation.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I meet singles in Mexico City?

Mexico City has the highest share of singles in the country (around 38% of people aged 12 and over, 2020 Census). The most effective routes to a serious relationship are the ones that filter for intention: curated events and trips for singles, private communities like TovLov Club and professional matchmaking.

How do I meet someone who wants a serious relationship?

Choose spaces where everyone shares that intention: events and trips for intentional singles, communities with an entry screening, or matchmaking, where a team vets each person before making an introduction.

What is a matchmaker?

A professional who gets to know you in depth through an interview, understands your values, lifestyle and relationship vision, and only introduces you to people who passed the same screening and are compatible with you.
