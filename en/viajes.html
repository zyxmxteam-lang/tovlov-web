Setting changes everything
i.Setting changes everything

Before the destination, we design the setting.

TovLov trips don’t start when you pack your bag. They start when you step out of your routine, your usual roles and the same old expectations. We create settings where you can relax, open up and connect as your most authentic self.

Trips with intention
ii.Trips with intention

You don’t travel with just anyone.

Every trip brings together a carefully selected group of conscious, intentional people aligned in their values. It isn’t social tourism: it’s real time together with people who know what they’re looking for and what they’re no longer willing to repeat. Affinity starts before takeoff.
