( 09 ) — Before you apply

What everyone asks first.

Have another question? Your 1:1 interview is the place to ask it, no strings attached.

How is TovLov different from a dating app?

It comes down to who decides. On a dating app, an algorithm shows you whoever is nearby; at TovLov, a human team gets to know you in an interview and introduces you only to people compatible in values, lifestyle and relationship vision. Our app is a tool for living that process, not a catalog of profiles to swipe through.

Do you accept everyone who applies?

No. Every application is reviewed individually. We look for people with a real intention to build a relationship and the willingness to commit to a serious process. That protects the quality of every introduction, including yours.

What is the selection process like?

You apply online, we review your application and, if there’s a fit with our method, we schedule a 1:1 interview with your matchmaker. From there we build your relationship profile and define your connection criteria together.

How confidential is the process?

Completely. There are no public profiles. Only your matchmaking team knows your information, and it’s never shared with anyone without your explicit permission.

Do you guarantee I’ll find a partner?

No honest service can guarantee love. What we do guarantee is a serious, human and personalized process that raises the quality of every connection and helps you decide with clarity.

What’s the difference between the Club and matchmaking?

The Club is a private community where you meet and talk with other intentional singles, at your own pace. Matchmaking is a 1:1 service: a matchmaker interviews you and introduces you to people selected for you. Many members start in the Club and later take the step into matchmaking.

When is the TovLov app launching?

Very soon. Active members and people on the waitlist get access first. Apply today and we’ll let you know the moment it’s live.
